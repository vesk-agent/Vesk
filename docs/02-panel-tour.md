# Panel tour

The panel is optional for self-hosted agents. It's a window into your agent, and it can pause it or flip its kill switch. It never sees your keys. In hosted mode it runs the agent for you, and [holder access](09-holder-access.md) applies.

Press **⌘K** (or Ctrl+K) anywhere to jump to a page or run an action.

Non-obvious settings have a small **?** next to their label. Point at a **?** for a short popover. On a wide screen, clicking it (or focusing the field) slides in a help panel on the right with what it does, a typical value and the trade-off; **Esc** or its **×** closes it. On a phone the **?** opens the same text as a popover.

## Agent

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Today's PnL as the page's one big number (its USD value under it), open positions, spend against your daily cap and the agent's state. Under it, **Top trades this week**: your best closed trades and the best of your copies of each tracked wallet, as avatar chips with the SOL they made. Then what needs you (launch-on-post drafts, copies waiting for Copy / Skip), realized PnL, open positions with quick sells, your automations and the live activity feed with each coin's face. |
| **Live** | What the agent is doing right now. A header with its state, uptime, mode, today's PnL, trades, wins and losses and spend, with Pause and Kill switch. **Ready to run**: wallet, RPC, model, X source, agent, Telegram and holder access, each Connected / Needs setup / Error, with quick fixes in a drawer (paste and test an RPC, add a model key) and one **Finish setup** button; a missing piece greys the steps it blocks. The pipeline **Watch → Check → Size → Execute → Exit → Log**, with candidates arriving on the left, the one being worked on moving along the line, filtered ones peeling off with their reason and executed ones landing in N ms. The header also says what it trades from (Snipe, Copy, Pulse, Launch on post, each on or off). Then the decision log (filter by Enter, Exit, Filtered, Skipped, Copied, Launched; **Follow live**) beside one card with three tabs: **Now trading** (each open trade with its source, entry → now, PnL, a price line and exits; Sell 50% / All on hover), **Strategy** (preset, buy size, early-volume trigger, rug and can-sell checks, exits, daily limit, sources and Rogue mode, saved as you change them) and **Session** (the day's result); then a raw `tail -f`. Press **L**. See [Live view](33-live.md). While Rogue mode runs, one line under the header shows the target, coins, budget and a countdown. |
| **Chat** | Plain English to rules, launches and launch-on-post rules. You see exactly what Vesk understood and confirm it. |
| **Pulse** | New coins in three columns: **New pairs**, **Final stretch** (bonding curve at 80% or more) and **Migrated**. Live from PumpPortal's public websocket, straight into your browser. Each column has its own filters (launchpad, keywords, audits such as dev %, top 10 %, snipers, insiders and bundled, plus metrics and socials), its own quick-buy amount and preset, a sound alert and pause on hover. Quick-buy goes through your trading wallet, preset, limits and guardrails. Click a row to open its coin page. Settings for the board: [Pulse settings](22-pulse-settings.md). How every number is computed: [Pulse](20-pulse.md). |
| **Tracker** | Tabs: the **Social feed** (the live X feed in columns: All, your groups, Mentions CA), **Callouts** (posts from your groups that contain a contract address), the **Wallet tracker** (live trades by wallets you track, with a PnL per wallet since the page opened) and *Accounts and buy rules*. Deploy on any post with **D**, Instant deploy with **⇧D**. Toasts and sounds per alert type are in Settings → Tracker & alerts. See [Tracker](03-tracker.md). |
| **Copy trade** | **PnL from copies** is the big number (closed copies plus open ones at the last price), with tracked wallets, copies today, detected → landed and the detection sources beside it. Two tabs: **Wallets and feed**, and a **Leaderboard** that ranks your tracked wallets by what copying them made you, or by win rate. Wallets you copy and how: mode (alert, confirm, auto), size, sells, entry filters (market cap at their buy, liquidity, age, dev, top 10, bundled, socials, venues, keywords), exits per copy and limits, with a default profile and per-wallet overrides. The live feed shows every trade they make as a sentence ("Night desk bought $GLINT · at $36K MC · 2m"), with their picture, a bought / sold chip and their SOL on the right, and under it what Vesk did (copied in N ms, or skipped and why) and a one-click manual copy. Click a wallet for its trades from your RPC, holdings and settings with the help rail. Groups, bulk edit, templates, CSV / JSON import and export. See [Copy trading](27-copy-trading.md). |
| **Rules** | Two tabs. **Snipe**: trigger → buy → exits. **Launch on post**: when an account posts, launch a coin from it. See [Snipe rules](04-snipe-rules.md) and [Launch on posts](05-launch-on-post.md). |
| **Positions** | The value of your open positions is the big number, with unrealized PnL since entry under it. **Open**: each position with where it came from (a copied wallet shows its picture and name; rules, launch on post, manual and Pulse buys show their glyph), its price since entry (entry line, exit targets on the edge, hover for the price), invested, value, unrealized PnL and progress to the next exit; sell chips appear on hover. **Realized**: today, 7 days and all, win rate, best and worst, a bar per day, and every closed position with its hold time and market cap at entry and exit. Plus trade history and quick buy by mint. |
| **Coin page** | `/app/coin/<mint>`, opened from any coin. A dense header led by the market cap in large type with its change (24h, or the coin's life when it's younger), then price, liquidity, supply, fees, curve and ATH; a chart you can zoom, pan and scale by dragging its axes, with B / S / DB / DS markers, a **Now** tag on the last price, your take-profit and stop levels as small coloured tags on the price axis, a range row under it (1H, 24H, 1W, 1M, All) and candles or a smooth line (candles from GeckoTerminal, source labelled); tabs for Trades, Positions, Orders, Holders, Top traders, Early buyers, Dev tokens and Callouts, with wallet icons and names; and a trade panel with Market, Limit and Adv. orders (migration, dev sell, DCA, TWAP, trailing, stop-loss). Market buys and sells are one wide solid green or red button that says *Processing* while the trade runs and shows a small "Bought 0.25 SOL of $GLINT ✓" chip when it lands; with **Slide to confirm** on, buys above your confirm size are a slide instead. For a coin you launched, the Positions tab adds **Your coin**: claim creator fees, burn (disclosed), buybacks and status posts. **PnL card** draws a share image in your browser. See [Coin page, orders and callouts](28-coin-page.md). |
| **Instant Trade** | Press **T** anywhere. A floating panel you can drag, shrink and collapse: buy chips buy from one wallet, sell chips sell a % from every wallet you tick, with a preset. Or switch it to the **amount view**: one big amount, 10 / 25 / 50 % / Max of what's available, the available balance and the fee, and one wide button. Its gear sets 1–2 rows of buy and sell chips, the preset, confirm size and which lines show. Limits and guardrails apply. See [Instant Trade](29-instant-trade.md). |
| **Trading settings** | The **P1** button in the top bar. Buy and sell presets P1/P2/P3 (slippage, priority fee, Jito tip, auto fee with a cap, MEV protection), quick amounts, the trading wallet, a confirmation above a size you choose, and hotkeys. Used by Pulse, the coin page, Instant Trade, the Tracker, rules and the CLI. See [Trading settings](21-trading-settings.md). |
| **Settings** | The gear in the top bar: layout, display, row elements, metric colours, hover previews, quick-buy behaviour and button styling, trading, blacklist, discovery protocols, pump live, tracker alerts, appearance (themes) and performance. See [Pulse settings](22-pulse-settings.md). |
| **Portfolio** | Total value as the big number with its 24h change, and a value chart (1D, 7D, 30D, All; hover for the value at any point), unrealized vs realized, allocation by token, wallet and source (a ring with each slice's picture, value and share), realized PnL for the last 14 days, holdings with their charts, realized PnL by day, best and worst trades, and each wallet's share. **Share PnL card** makes a 1200×630 image of your realized results (7 days, 30 days or all time, with or without SOL amounts) in your browser; **Export CSV** saves your holdings or closed positions. The value chart is built from snapshots the server takes every 15 minutes, so it starts when your account does. |
| **Claim** | Under Agent, after Portfolio. What you can claim now, **per asset** (SOL, and each custom pair's asset, with ≈ SOL where priced), then one row per coin you launched: pair, claimable, claimed to date and the action. **Claim**, **Claim all** (one claim per asset, paid in that asset) and each claim's progress in one notification. Below it, **Cleanup**: wallets with empty token accounts and **Close and reclaim**. See [Claim](39-claim.md). |
| **Launch** | Three ways in: **Launch it yourself** (sign with your own browser wallet), **Launch and hand to the agent** (then exits, fee claims and disclosed buybacks run inside your limits) and **Let the agent launch on posts**. The flow: Chain & launchpad → Token (the coin as a live preview card; quote pair tiles and More pairs, the custom pairs by category; on a custom pair a 0.05-1% creator fee paid in that asset, with presets, Cashback and a fee split; none on SOL) → Settings → Review. Plus **Ask Vesk to do it all** and your launches. Archive launches to tidy the list; nothing on chain changes. See [Launch flow and presets](06-launch-and-presets.md). |

## Setup

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Wallets** | The SOL across your wallets is the big number; the change under it is your total value (wallets and coins) over 24 hours, labelled as such. Your wallets, named **Main**, **Wallet 1**, **Wallet 2**… until you rename them in place; imported wallets, fund from main, return all. Pair a self-hosted agent. Tabs for **Folders** and the **Address book** (labels, colour tags, images, track, JSON/CSV import and export). Closing empty token accounts moved to **Claim**. See [Wallets](08-wallets.md) and [Wallet names, labels and icons](30-wallet-labels.md). |
| **Launch presets** | Saved launch settings: dev buy (0 for a free launch), fees, links, image source, naming, exits including dev-sell timers. |
| **Connections** | RPC with a latency test, model and API keys, X source, agent mode (paper or live), Telegram. |
| **Strategy & safety** | How the agent picks, sizes and leaves new coins, in six tabs: **Entry** (the early-volume trigger, market-cap, liquidity and age bounds, the buy size, scale-in), **Rug check** (bundled, dev, top 10, snipers, insiders, holders, dev history, authorities, LP; each switchable; bundled coins refused unless you allow them), **Honeypot** (the can-sell check), **Exits** (take-profit ladder, stop-loss, trailing, timed and no-volume sells), **Hard limits** (per trade, per day, open positions, buys per minute and per day, reserve, the kill switch and the guardrails you can't turn off) and **Rogue mode**. A preset row (Safe, Balanced, Degen, Rogue), a search across every field, a "?" on every one, Export and Import. See [Strategy & safety](34-strategy.md), [Rogue mode](35-rogue-mode.md) and [Honeypot check](36-honeypot.md). |
| **Holder access** | Linked wallets, $VESK held vs the minimum. Hosted only. |
| **Performance** | Low resource, Auto or Full speed, with live frame and update rates from this tab. See [Performance profiles](31-performance.md). |
| **Logs** | Every decision with its reason, filterable, exportable as CSV. |

Self-hosted: trades, sends, burns and rent recovery you start in the panel are queued for your agent, which signs them on your machine within about 2 seconds.

The block at the top of the sidebar is your agent and its state (online, paused, paper, kill switch, offline); hover it for the mode and host, uptime, RPC latency, version, model, X source and active rules. Every page is in the sidebar, Launch presets, Holder access, Performance and Logs included (and in ⌘K); on a short window the list scrolls on its own while the wallet and Docs stay put.

The top bar shows where you are, a **Setup** indicator while setup isn't finished ("Setup 3 of 5" with a thin bar; click it for the five steps: Wallet, RPC, Model, Rules, Live), today's PnL (hover it for your wallet balance), then Trade, your active preset, Pause, settings and the kill switch. The status bar shows service health and latency and your display's refresh rate (see [Performance profiles](31-performance.md#in-the-panel)); hover the health item for the SOL price, RPC and model, holder access and links.

**Notifications** show at the top centre, under the top bar, as a list: every card in full, newest on top, three at a time and **+N more** (pointing at one pauses the timers). Settings → Notifications → Style → **Stacked** makes them a deck that opens on hover. A trade is one card from the click to the result: *Signing, Sent*, then *Landed in N ms* with what you spent and got, the average price, fees, the slot and the transaction, or *Failed* with the reason and Retry. After a buy, **Sell 50%** and **Sell all** are on the card. Wallet-tracker bursts merge into one card ("3 buys from Night desk"). Swipe a card away or press **Esc**. The **bell** in the top bar (or **N**) opens the notification centre: every notification, Today and Earlier, with filters and Mark all read. They never cover Instant Trade, a dialog or the Buy button. Each kind has its own pop-up, sound and desktop setting under **Settings → Notifications**, with Do not disturb. See [Notifications](32-notifications.md).

**Big numbers** (today's PnL, totals, a coin's market cap) roll their digits when they change; widths stay fixed so nothing moves. Sections fade up once when a page opens. Both are off with reduced motion, on the Low resource profile, and (rolling digits) with **Settings → Appearance → Rolling digits** off.
