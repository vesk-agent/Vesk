# FAQ

**Is Vesk free?**
The agent is open source (MIT) and free for everyone: CLI, Telegram bot, MCP server. You pay for your RPC, your model, network fees and any tips your rules set.

**What does holding $VESK do?**
Holding at least 500,000 $VESK, in wallets you verify by signing a message, unlocks the hosted panel and API. The self-hosted agent doesn't need it. No revenue share, no promises. Only buy what you can afford to lose, and verify the contract address. See [Holder access](09-holder-access.md).

**What is launch on post?**
A rule that watches accounts you pick. When one posts, Vesk drafts a coin from the post and launches it with your preset, automatically or after you confirm. Every coin says it's inspired by the post and not affiliated with the poster. See [Launch on posts](05-launch-on-post.md).

**Can it really be first?**
It's built for it: the image fetch starts the instant the post arrives, connections stay warm, and the launch is one Jito bundle. Whether you're first depends on your X source, RPC and everyone else. Vesk measures post received → landed on your setup and shows it, and doesn't promise a number.

**What does a free launch cost?**
No dev buy, but network and launchpad fees still apply: about ~0.02 SOL on pump.fun today. Check pump.fun's current fees.

**Why would a dev sell after 5 seconds?**
Many buyers prefer coins where the dev holds no supply. A timed dev sell does that on a schedule, and every dev sell is public in the treasury report. See [Exits](07-exits.md).

**Does Vesk hold my funds?**
Self-hosted: never. Every transaction, manual or automated, is signed on your machine. Hosted: a separate trading wallet, encrypted server-side, that you can withdraw from or export any time.

**Is the login phrase a wallet?**
No. It only signs you in to the panel. Never type a wallet seed phrase into Vesk.

**Will it make me money?**
Nobody can promise that. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Start in paper mode, then use small sizes.
